HAProxy has been deployed.

To get the LoadBalancer IP/hostname, run:

  kubectl get service {{ include "haproxy.fullname" . }} --namespace {{ .Release.Namespace }} -o jsonpath='{.status.loadBalancer.ingress[0].ip}'

Or if using hostname:

  kubectl get service {{ include "haproxy.fullname" . }} --namespace {{ .Release.Namespace }} -o jsonpath='{.status.loadBalancer.ingress[0].hostname}'

You will need to configure your Cloudflare Tunnel ingress to point to this IP/hostname on port {{ .Values.service.port }}.
